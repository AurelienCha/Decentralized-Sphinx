\begin{abstract}
%
Sphinx is a packet format widely used in decentralized networks such as Nym, Katzenpost, and the Lightning network. 
It provides strong security and privacy properties against network adversaries and compromised nodes who aim to identify communicating parties by correlating the input with the output traffic.
However, Sphinx is vulnerable against malicious clients who exploit the centralized packet construction to deviate from the prescribed routing protocol.
In this paper, we propose DSphinx, a decentralized variant of the Sphinx packet format that distributes header construction among multiple semi-trusted third parties.  
We provide a formal analysis of the main security properties of our decentralized Sphinx packet format in the presence of network adversaries, corrupted mixnodes and semi-trusted third parties.
Additionally, we implement our scheme in Python and evaluate the unlinkability property using the NIST SP 800-22 statistical randomness test suite.  
Our empirical results show that our decentralized scheme achieves unlinkability passing more statistical tests than the original Sphinx format.

\keywords{Sphinx \and Mixnet \and Decentralized networks.}
\end{abstract}
